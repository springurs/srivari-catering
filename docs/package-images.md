## New package photos

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool using the imagegen skill. Generated originals are preserved in Codex's generated-images directory; the website thumbnails are converted to 256 × 256 WebP.

### power-lunch

Saved asset: `public/images/package-power-lunch.webp`.

Prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square food photo used as a small package thumbnail icon on the Srivari Indian vegetarian catering website. Photorealistic editorial food photography, appetizing authentic textures, warm natural light, ivory tabletop and restrained gold accents. A clear central food subject that reads well cropped to a small square image. Square 1:1 framing. No lettering, logos, watermark, people, meat, seafood or eggs. Subject: an elegant modern Indian lunch bowl, white ceramic bowl of jeera rice topped with golden paneer tikka cubes, fresh cucumber and tomato salad, seasonal vegetables and a small dollop of mint chutney. A small masala corn and peanut chaat cup beside it. View from 45 degrees above, close framing with the entire main bowl prominent and sharply focused, soft background.
